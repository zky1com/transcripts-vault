# Geoffrey Hinton - Public institutions are entering frontier AI governance with enormous uncertainty and no guaranteed path to safety

## Position
Geoffrey Hinton says society is entering frontier AI governance under enormous uncertainty and without any guaranteed path to safety.

## Evidence Cited
- Hinton says he cannot see a path that guarantees safety.
- He says humanity is dealing with things it has never dealt with before.
- He argues that governments need regulation and coordinated action now because the downside of getting it wrong is severe.

## Logic Chain
1. Hinton frames frontier AI as a novel governance problem with little historical precedent.
2. He says existing institutions do not have a clear, reliable safety framework in place.
3. Therefore the claim captures his warning about institutional underpreparedness.
