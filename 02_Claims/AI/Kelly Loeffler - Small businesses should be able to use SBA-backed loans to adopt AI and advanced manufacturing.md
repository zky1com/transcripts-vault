# Kelly Loeffler - Small businesses should be able to use SBA-backed loans to adopt AI and advanced manufacturing

## Position
Kelly Loeffler argues that government-backed small-business lending should support AI implementation and smart manufacturing.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says she rewrote SBA rules so loan proceeds can be used for AI implementation and advanced manufacturing.
- She describes small businesses and solopreneurs as a key driver of the AI boom.
- She presents lending access as a way to spread the benefits of AI adoption beyond large firms.

## Logic Chain
1. Small businesses often lack capital to adopt new tools quickly.
2. SBA-backed loans can lower that financing barrier.
3. So expanding eligible loan uses can accelerate AI diffusion into the real economy.
