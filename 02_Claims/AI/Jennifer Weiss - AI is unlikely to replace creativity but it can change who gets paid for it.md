# Jennifer Weiss - AI is unlikely to replace creativity but it can change who gets paid for it

## Position
Jennifer Weiss says AI is unlikely to replace creativity but it can change who gets paid for it.

## Evidence Cited
- Weiss says AI will not replace creativity itself.
- She argues the technology may still alter payment and compensation flows around creative work.

## Logic Chain
1. Creative origination and economic capture are not identical.
2. AI can mediate distribution, copying, and monetization even if humans still originate ideas.
3. Therefore compensation can shift even without full creative replacement.
