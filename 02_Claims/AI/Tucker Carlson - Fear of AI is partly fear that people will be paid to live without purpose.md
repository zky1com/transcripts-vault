# Tucker Carlson - Fear of AI is partly fear that people will be paid to live without purpose

## Position
Tucker Carlson says a major source of AI anxiety is the idea that technological unemployment would leave people idle and purposeless.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says what bothers him most about the AI conversation is the UBI framing.
- He argues that people, especially men, need to feel useful.
- He says paying people to be content without purpose would be socially corrosive.

## Logic Chain
1. AI fears are partly about social meaning, not only technical risk.
2. UBI implies a future where contribution is disconnected from livelihood.
3. So anti-AI sentiment can be driven by cultural and moral concerns about usefulness.
