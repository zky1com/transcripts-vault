# Jennifer Weiss - AI is creating serious publishing risks around name misuse and unauthorized competitive books

## Position
Jennifer Weiss says AI is creating serious publishing risks around name misuse and unauthorized competitive books.

## Evidence Cited
- Weiss describes cases where someone used an author's name on an AI-generated competing book.
- She presents that as a major current threat facing the industry.

## Logic Chain
1. AI lowers the cost of generating derivative or imitative book products.
2. That makes unauthorized use of names and adjacent titles easier.
3. Therefore AI increases publishing risk around attribution and competition abuse.
