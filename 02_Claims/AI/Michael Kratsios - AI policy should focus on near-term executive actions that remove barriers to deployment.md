# Michael Kratsios - AI policy should focus on near-term executive actions that remove barriers to deployment

## Position
Michael Kratsios says the practical AI agenda should emphasize concrete executive actions that speed deployment.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says the plan is an action plan focused on things government can accomplish in the next six to nine months.
- He says agencies should create an environment where AI-enabled products can thrive rather than be hindered.
- He frames the current need as removing barriers across sectors touched by AI.

## Logic Chain
1. Many AI bottlenecks are regulatory and operational.
2. The executive branch can address some of them without waiting for sweeping new legislation.
3. So a useful AI policy should prioritize fast, actionable changes.
