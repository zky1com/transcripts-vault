# David Sacks - America cannot win the AI race without out-innovating rivals and building more domestic infrastructure

## Position
David Sacks argues that American AI leadership depends on innovation plus domestic buildout, not innovation alone.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says the United States has to win the AI race and cannot regulate its way to victory.
- He outlines three pillars: innovation, infrastructure, and ecosystem scale.
- He says America needs more domestic data centers, energy, and manufacturing capacity.

## Logic Chain
1. AI leadership is a geopolitical and economic contest.
2. Winning requires both better models and the infrastructure to deploy them.
3. So domestic buildout is part of the competitive requirement, not an optional add-on.
