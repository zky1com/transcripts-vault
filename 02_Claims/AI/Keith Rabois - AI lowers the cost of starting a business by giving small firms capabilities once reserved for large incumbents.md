# Keith Rabois - AI lowers the cost of starting a business by giving small firms capabilities once reserved for large incumbents

## Position
Keith Rabois says AI acts like rocket fuel for entrepreneurship because it gives small firms access to tools and expertise that used to require large organizations.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says AI gives startups access to information, product-building ability, and administrative savings.
- He argues that entrepreneurs can now compete with large incumbents on a more level playing field.
- He says this lowers the risk and cost of starting a business.

## Logic Chain
1. Incumbents used to have structural advantages in expertise and back-office scale.
2. AI compresses those advantages by automating or cheapening them.
3. So smaller firms can compete in categories that previously favored large companies.
