# Dan Morehead - Many large alternative asset firms still have zero crypto exposure

## Position
Dan Morehead says many very large alternative asset firms still have no exposure to bitcoin or crypto.

## Evidence Cited
- He says he recently shared a panel with heads of large listed alternative-asset firms.
- He says almost all of them were negative on bitcoin and crypto and had no exposure.
- He cites that lack of ownership as a reason he remains bullish on long-term adoption.

## Logic Chain
1. Morehead describes a large institutional group with little or no crypto allocation.
2. He treats that underownership as evidence that the trade is not saturated.
3. Therefore the claim captures his institutional-positioning argument.
