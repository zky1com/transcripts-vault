# Dan Morehead - A strategic Bitcoin reserve race among nation-states could emerge within two to three years

## Position
Dan Morehead says a strategic Bitcoin reserve race among nation-states could emerge within the next two to three years.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the United States is establishing a strategic bitcoin reserve.
- He says countries aligned with the US are also acquiring bitcoin.
- He argues antagonistic countries may decide it is safer to hold bitcoin than dollar assets vulnerable to sanctions.

## Logic Chain
1. Morehead identifies early sovereign bitcoin reserve behavior.
2. He argues geopolitical incentives could spread that behavior to more states.
3. Therefore he expects a nation-state accumulation race to emerge.
