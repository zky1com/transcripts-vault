# Warris Bokhari - Appealed denials are overturned at high rates because many original denials are weak

## Position
Warris Bokhari says appealed insurance denials are overturned at high rates because many original denials are weak.

## Evidence Cited
- He cites overturn rates from about 50% to above 70% and even into the 90s in some areas.
- He argues insurers rely on people giving up before appealing.

## Logic Chain
1. Bokhari says most denials go unchallenged.
2. When patients do appeal, many denials are reversed.
3. Therefore he infers the initial denials are often poor-quality decisions.
