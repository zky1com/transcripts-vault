# Stu Elman - Venture capital follows a law of thirds where many companies fail and returns depend on a small minority of winners

## Position
Stu Elman says venture capital follows a law of thirds where many companies fail and the economics depend on a small minority of winners.

## Evidence Cited
- Elman says roughly a third of investments lose all their money and another third fail to return capital.
- He says venture outcomes therefore depend on the final third producing the big successes.

## Logic Chain
1. Elman breaks venture outcomes into three rough buckets.
2. Most companies either fail outright or underperform.
3. Therefore returns depend heavily on a small winner cohort.
