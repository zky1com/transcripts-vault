# Stu Elman - Venture firms often die slowly because bad funds linger even when they stop putting real money to work

## Position
Stu Elman says venture firms often die slowly because bad funds linger even when they stop putting real money to work.

## Evidence Cited
- Elman says zombie funds can remain nominally alive while doing little actual investing.
- He describes a slow decay rather than sudden firm failure.

## Logic Chain
1. Venture firms can remain responsible for old portfolios even after fundraising deteriorates.
2. That lets weak firms continue existing without meaningful new deployment.
3. Therefore venture-firm failure often happens gradually.
