# Stu Elman - Venture firms have to keep investing through up and down markets rather than only during boom periods

## Position
Stu Elman says venture firms have to keep investing through up and down markets rather than only during boom periods.

## Evidence Cited
- Elman says markets go up and down but firms still have to build great companies and keep making investments.
- He frames continuity through cycles as part of competent venture practice.

## Logic Chain
1. Venture investing spans long holding periods.
2. Market regimes change before those investments mature.
3. Therefore firms have to operate across both boom and bust conditions.
