# Stu Elman - Markups can create the illusion that a venture fund returned capital when it really has not

## Position
Stu Elman says private-market markups can create the illusion that a venture fund returned capital when it really has not.

## Evidence Cited
- Elman describes companies being marked up sharply without actual cash realization.
- He says those apparent gains can reverse later, leaving the fund without real returned capital.

## Logic Chain
1. Venture valuations are often updated from private financing rounds rather than liquid market exits.
2. Those marks can overstate realized economic success.
3. Therefore a fund can look returned on paper while still lacking actual cash distributions.
