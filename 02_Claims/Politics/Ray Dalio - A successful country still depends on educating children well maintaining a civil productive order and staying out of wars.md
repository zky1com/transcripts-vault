# Ray Dalio - A successful country still depends on educating children well maintaining a civil productive order and staying out of wars

## Position
Ray Dalio argues that a successful country still depends on educating children well maintaining a civil productive order and staying out of wars.

## Evidence Cited
- He says those are the three recurring ingredients that countries need in order to succeed.
- He links education to productivity and civility to social function.
- He says civil and international conflict break that foundation down.

## Logic Chain
1. Societies need capable people social order and peace to convert resources into durable prosperity.
2. Dalio says history shows those three conditions repeatedly.
3. That supports his claim about the core ingredients of national success.
