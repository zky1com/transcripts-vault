# David Sacks - The media invented the Bessent put partly to avoid giving Trump credit for a market rebound

## Position
David Sacks argues that the media invented the Bessent put partly to avoid giving Trump credit for a market rebound.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the same outlets that blamed Trump for selloffs became reluctant to credit him for the rally.
- He argues they highlighted Scott Bessent individually to avoid praising the administration as a whole.
- He says this is part of a broader pattern of selectively valorizing cabinet members instead of acknowledging Trump.'s role.

## Logic Chain
1. If media narratives assign downside to Trump but redirect upside to subordinates, the narrative serves an attribution goal rather than a neutral one.
2. Sacks says that is what happened with the Bessent-put framing.
3. That supports his claim that the phrase was partly created to deny Trump rebound credit.
