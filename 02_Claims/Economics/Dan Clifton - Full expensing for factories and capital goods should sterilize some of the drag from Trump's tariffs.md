# Dan Clifton - Full expensing for factories and capital goods should sterilize some of the drag from Trump's tariffs

## Position
Dan Clifton says full expensing for factories and capital goods should sterilize some of the drag from Trump's tariffs.

## Evidence Cited
- Clifton says the business tax provisions are meant to sterilize the tariff drag.
- He highlights 100% expensing for factories and capital goods as the mechanism.

## Logic Chain
1. Tariffs raise costs and can slow investment.
2. Immediate expensing lowers the effective after-tax cost of investment.
3. Therefore the tax provisions offset some of the tariff burden.
