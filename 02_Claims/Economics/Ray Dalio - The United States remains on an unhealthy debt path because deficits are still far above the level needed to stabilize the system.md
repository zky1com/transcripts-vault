# Ray Dalio - The United States remains on an unhealthy debt path because deficits are still far above the level needed to stabilize the system

## Position
Ray Dalio argues that the United States remains on an unhealthy debt path because deficits are still far above the level needed to stabilize the system.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the government is still spending far more than it takes in and rolling over massive amounts of maturing debt.
- He repeats that roughly 3% of GDP would be the stabilizing zone, while current deficits are much higher.
- He describes the debt-service burden as plaque that squeezes out productive spending.

## Logic Chain
1. A debt system becomes unstable when servicing and rollover needs grow faster than the income base can comfortably support.
2. Dalio says the United States is still well inside that unhealthy zone.
3. That supports his claim that the country remains on an unsustainable debt path.
