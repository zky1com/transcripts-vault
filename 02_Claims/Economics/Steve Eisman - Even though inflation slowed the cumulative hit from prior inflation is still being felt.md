# Steve Eisman - Even though inflation slowed the cumulative hit from prior inflation is still being felt

## Position
Steve Eisman says that even though inflation slowed the cumulative hit from prior inflation is still being felt.

## Evidence Cited
- He distinguishes between the current inflation rate and the accumulated price level.
- He argues households still feel the earlier increase in prices.

## Logic Chain
1. Lower inflation only means prices are rising more slowly.
2. It does not reverse earlier price increases.
3. Eisman therefore says the cumulative hit is still being felt.
