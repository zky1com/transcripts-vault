# Andrew Ross Sorkin - Policy mistakes can deepen a downturn once financial excess is already built into the system

## Position
Andrew Ross Sorkin says fragile financial conditions become more dangerous when policymakers add unhelpful moves on top of them.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says policymakers knew there was a problem but failed to tighten when needed.
- He points to Hoover's tax increases and tariff decisions as part of the worsening sequence.
- The discussion treats policy error as an amplifier rather than the sole cause.

## Logic Chain
1. Financial excess can leave a system vulnerable before the break.
2. Bad policy choices during that vulnerable period intensify the damage.
3. So downturn analysis has to include policy response as part of the causal chain.
