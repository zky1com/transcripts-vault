# Steve Eisman - The financial crisis came from too much leverage, a collapsing asset class, systemically important holders, and derivatives

## Position
Steve Eisman says the financial crisis came from too much leverage, a collapsing asset class, systemically important firms holding that asset class, and derivatives.

## Evidence Cited
- Eisman gives a four-part summary of the crisis: leverage, asset collapse, systemically important holders, and derivatives.
- He says the first three features appear in most financial blowups and derivatives were the key extra factor in 2008.

## Logic Chain
1. Eisman reduces the crisis to four interacting elements.
2. He argues the combination of those elements explains why the crisis became systemic.
3. Therefore the claim captures his compact crisis framework.
