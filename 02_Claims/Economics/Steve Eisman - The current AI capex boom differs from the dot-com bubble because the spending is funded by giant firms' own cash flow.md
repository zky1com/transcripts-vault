## Position
Steve Eisman says the current AI capex boom differs from the dot-com bubble because the spending is funded by giant firms' own cash flow.

## Evidence Cited

- He says today's AI spend comes from the largest firms on earth using their own cash flow.

## Logic Chain
1. Eisman compares the financing base of the AI buildout with the financing base of the dot-com era.
