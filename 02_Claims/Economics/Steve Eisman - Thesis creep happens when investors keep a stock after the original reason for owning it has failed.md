# Steve Eisman - Thesis creep happens when investors keep a stock after the original reason for owning it has failed

## Position
Steve Eisman defines thesis creep as the mistake of holding a position after the core reason for owning it has broken.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript explicitly defines thesis creep in those terms.
- He describes investors changing the story instead of admitting the thesis failed.
- He presents it as one of the most common investing errors.

## Logic Chain
1. Every investment starts with a specific reason.
2. If that reason fails and the position is retained anyway, discipline has broken down.
3. So thesis creep is the act of letting attachment replace the original thesis.
