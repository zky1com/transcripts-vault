# Steve Eisman - Because compensation tracked volume no one had an economic incentive to slow the gravy train

## Position
Steve Eisman says that because compensation tracked volume no one had an economic incentive to slow the gravy train.

## Evidence Cited
- Eisman ties the persistence of bad lending directly to incentive design.
- He says nobody had an economic reason to slow the system down.

## Logic Chain
1. Participants were rewarded for keeping the machine running.
2. Slowing issuance would have reduced their income.
3. Therefore the system lacked internal brakes.
