# Chris Verrone - The market's sharp rebound reflects how sentiment inverted from euphoric to extremely washed out

## Position
Chris Verrone says the market's sharp rebound reflects how sentiment flipped from euphoric at the start of the year to extremely washed out by April.

## Evidence Cited
- Verrone says flows, surveys, and put-call data were euphoric early in the year.
- He says those measures fully inverted by the April low.

## Logic Chain
1. Verrone frames the selloff as a sentiment reset from excess optimism to deep pessimism.
2. That reset created the conditions for a powerful rally.
3. Therefore the claim captures his explanation for the rebound.
