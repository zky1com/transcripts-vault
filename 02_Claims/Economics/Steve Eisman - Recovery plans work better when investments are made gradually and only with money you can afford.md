# Steve Eisman - Recovery plans work better when investments are made gradually and only with money you can afford

## Position
Steve Eisman advises investors to rebuild steadily rather than through large emotionally driven bets.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says to invest right now with what you can, no matter how small.
- He says to use only money you can afford.
- He recommends continuing that pattern over time.

## Logic Chain
1. Regular small investing lowers the pressure to time the market.
2. Using only affordable capital limits the chance of forced decisions.
3. So gradual disciplined investing is a better recovery method than lumpy speculation.
