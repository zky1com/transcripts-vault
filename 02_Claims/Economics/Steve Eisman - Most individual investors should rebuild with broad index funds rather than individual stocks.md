# Steve Eisman - Most individual investors should rebuild with broad index funds rather than individual stocks

## Position

- He explicitly recommends the S&P 500 index.

## Logic Chain

2. Broad indices lower that risk and simplify the process.
3. So most individuals are better served by rebuilding through index exposure.
