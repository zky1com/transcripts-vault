# David Friedberg - AI may push productive capacity beyond humanity's ability to consume the output

## Position
David Friedberg argues that AI may push productive capacity beyond humanity's ability to consume the output.

## Evidence Cited
- He says AI creates a profound jump in leverage on both human time and capital.
- He says the new question is whether there is an upper limit on consumptive capacity.
- He says we may soon be able to make more than people can actually absorb.

## Logic Chain
1. If productivity rises much faster than consumption can expand, output can outrun demand.
2. Friedberg says AI may create exactly that unprecedented condition.
3. That supports his claim that productive capacity could exceed human consumption.
