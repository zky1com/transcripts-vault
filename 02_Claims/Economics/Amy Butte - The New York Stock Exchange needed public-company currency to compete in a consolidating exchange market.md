# Amy Butte - The New York Stock Exchange needed public-company currency to compete in a consolidating exchange market

## Position
Amy Butte says the NYSE needed public status because competing exchanges already had acquisition currency during an industry consolidation wave.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says rival exchanges were public and had much larger market capitalizations.
- She explains that the NYSE could not keep competing without that currency.
- The discussion ties public status directly to strategic flexibility in consolidation.

## Logic Chain
1. Consolidating industries reward players that can issue stock for acquisitions.
2. Private companies lack that same currency.
3. So the NYSE needed to become public to stay competitive.
