# Steve Eisman - The existing home market is locked because sellers will not give up 3% mortgages and buyers cannot afford 6% rates

## Position
Steve Eisman says that the existing home market is locked because sellers will not give up 3% mortgages and buyers cannot afford 6% rates.

## Evidence Cited
- He contrasts pandemic-era mortgages with current borrowing costs.
- He uses that gap to explain why housing transactions are frozen.

## Logic Chain
1. Existing owners are anchored to very low mortgage rates.
2. Current buyers face much higher rates.
3. Eisman therefore says the market is locked because neither side can transact easily.
