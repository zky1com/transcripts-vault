# Amy Butte - A company cannot credibly go public while relying on budget plugs instead of real financial planning

## Position
Amy Butte says a company cannot meet public-company standards if its internal budgets are being padded with fictional plugs.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript recounts the NYSE budget showing profitability only because a plug had been inserted.
- She describes this as a sign that the real planning process was inadequate.
- The anecdote is used to illustrate how far the organization had to mature.

## Logic Chain
1. Public markets depend on credible financial reporting.
2. Budget plugs undermine the reliability of those numbers.
3. So a business using them is not yet ready for public scrutiny.
