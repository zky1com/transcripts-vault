# Steve Eisman - Growth stocks are usually hurt more than staple-like businesses when interest rates rise

## Position
Steve Eisman says rising rates usually hurt growth stocks more than staple-like businesses.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says higher rates pressure growth valuations through discount-rate mechanics.
- He contrasts that with more stable sectors that tend to hold up better.
- The masterclass presents rate sensitivity as uneven across sectors.

## Logic Chain
1. Growth stocks depend more heavily on earnings expected far in the future.
2. Higher discount rates reduce the present value of those distant cash flows more sharply.
3. So growth stocks usually take a larger valuation hit when rates rise.
