# Amy Butte - Going public forces a company to change both its controls and its internal mindset

## Position
Amy Butte argues that IPO preparation is cultural as well as procedural because people must start thinking like managers of a public for-profit company.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says they had to ask what the company would look like as a for-profit entity.
- She says they needed to change how people inside the organization thought and acted.
- The discussion presents mindset change as necessary for lasting readiness.

## Logic Chain
1. Controls can be installed formally.
2. But public-company behavior also depends on how people think about the business.
3. So successful IPO prep requires both system change and mindset change.
