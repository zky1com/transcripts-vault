# Ray Dalio - America's profit-driven AI model faces a real challenge from a Chinese approach that prioritizes broad usage over profits

## Position
Ray Dalio argues that America's profit-driven AI model faces a real challenge from a Chinese approach that prioritizes broad usage over profits.

## Evidence Cited
- He says China may treat AI more like electricity and make it broadly available or open source rather than optimize for profits first.
- He says Chinese technology is not far behind the United States.
- He says that creates a difficult competitive problem for American companies that still need to earn back huge investments.

## Logic Chain
1. A rival system can pressure a profit model if it spreads similar-quality technology widely without demanding the same returns.
2. Dalio says China may be taking that route in AI.
3. That supports his claim that the US profit-led model faces a serious challenge from China's usage-led approach.
