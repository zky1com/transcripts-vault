# Steve Eisman - Risk-weighted capital frameworks encouraged banks to load up on assets that only looked safe by historical measures

## Position
Steve Eisman says risk-weighted capital frameworks encouraged banks to load up on assets that only looked safe by historical measures.

## Evidence Cited
- Eisman says leverage looked flat on a risk-weighted basis even while pure mathematical leverage tripled.
- He says the framework incentivized banks to own assets with low historical risk weights.
- He argues those risk weights were backward-looking and failed when underwriting standards changed.

## Logic Chain
1. Eisman says regulatory capital math understated real leverage.
2. That understatement pushed banks toward assets that appeared safe only under stale historical assumptions.
3. Therefore the claim captures his critique of risk-weighted capital design.
