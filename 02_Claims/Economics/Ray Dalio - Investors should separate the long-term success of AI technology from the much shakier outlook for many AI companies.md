# Ray Dalio - Investors should separate the long-term success of AI technology from the much shakier outlook for many AI companies

## Position
Ray Dalio argues that investors should separate the long-term success of AI technology from the much shakier outlook for many AI companies.

## Evidence Cited
- He says people in bubbles often think they are betting on the technology when they are actually betting on specific companies.
- He says a lot of companies will not survive even if the technology itself goes on to transform the economy.
- He compares this pattern to earlier tech booms where the innovation endured but many firms did not.

## Logic Chain
1. A durable technology can still produce weak investment results if too many firms compete away profits or fail outright.
2. Dalio says AI fits that pattern.
3. That supports his claim that technology success and company success should be separated analytically.
