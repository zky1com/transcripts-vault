# Steve Eisman - Taking a company public starts by redesigning it to operate like a real for-profit public business

## Position
Steve Eisman frames IPO preparation as an operating redesign, not just a financing event.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript opens by asking what it takes to make a private company ready to go public.
- The conversation emphasizes that public markets demand different discipline than private ownership.
- Eisman repeatedly treats the transition as organizational, not merely transactional.

## Logic Chain

2. A private company must change how it operates to meet that standard.
3. So going public begins with redesigning the business for public-company conditions.
