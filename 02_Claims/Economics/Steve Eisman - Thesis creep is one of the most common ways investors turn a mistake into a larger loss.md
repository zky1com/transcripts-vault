# Steve Eisman - Thesis creep is one of the most common ways investors turn a mistake into a larger loss

## Position
Steve Eisman says thesis creep is a common failure mode in which investors refuse to exit after their original thesis breaks.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says thesis creep is the bane of every investor.
- He defines it as keeping a stock after the thesis no longer works.
- He links it to emotional attachment and unwillingness to admit error.

## Logic Chain
1. Investors naturally resist admitting mistakes.
2. That resistance encourages them to rewrite the story instead of exiting.
3. So thesis creep often converts a manageable error into a larger one.
