# David Sacks - Any wealth tax would likely expand over time from billionaires to much broader groups

## Position
David Sacks argues that any wealth tax would likely expand over time from billionaires to much broader groups.

## Evidence Cited
- He says once government crosses the line from no wealth tax to any wealth tax, the precedent is established.
- He compares it to the historical expansion of income taxation from a narrow levy to a broad one.
- He says the one-time framing is not credible because deficits and spending pressures will persist.

## Logic Chain
1. Tax categories created for a narrow target often broaden once the underlying fiscal appetite remains.
2. Sacks says California would not stop at a one-time billionaire levy if the mechanism worked.
3. That supports his claim that a wealth tax would spread beyond its initial target group.
