# Steve Eisman - The stock market's gains have mainly enriched upper-income households while the bottom 80 to 90 percent have not kept up

## Position
Steve Eisman says that the stock market's gains have mainly enriched upper-income households while the bottom 80 to 90 percent have not kept up.

## Evidence Cited
- He links stock-market wealth gains to higher-income households.
- He contrasts that with the weaker experience of most consumers.

## Logic Chain
1. Equity ownership is concentrated toward the top of the income distribution.
2. Rising markets therefore benefit upper-income households disproportionately.
3. Eisman concludes that most consumers have not kept up.
