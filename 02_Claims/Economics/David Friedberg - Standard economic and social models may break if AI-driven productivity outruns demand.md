# David Friedberg - Standard economic and social models may break if AI-driven productivity outruns demand

## Position
David Friedberg argues that standard economic and social models may break if AI-driven productivity outruns demand.

## Evidence Cited
- He says this would be something humanity has not faced before.
- He says general economic productivity and social models start to break under that scenario.
- He connects the issue directly to uncertainty about what everyone will do in such a world.

## Logic Chain
1. Economic models assume some relationship between greater productivity and absorbable output.
2. Friedberg says AI may violate that assumption.
3. That supports his claim that existing models may break if demand cannot keep up.
