# Chamath Palihapitiya - Gold is rallying because new buyers and distrust of central-bank policy matter more than any single macro story

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that gold is rallying because new buyers and distrust of central-bank policy matter more than any single macro story.

## Evidence Cited
- He points to stablecoin-linked gold demand through products like Tether Gold.
- He says central banks are rebalancing reserves while macro funds are losing confidence in policy frameworks.
- He rejects one-cause explanations and frames the move as a convergence of several demand drivers.

## Logic Chain
1. When multiple new buyer classes emerge at once, price action can be driven by demand structure more than by a single narrative headline.
2. Chamath says that is what is happening in gold.
3. That supports his claim that the rally reflects broad buyer demand and policy distrust rather than one macro story.
