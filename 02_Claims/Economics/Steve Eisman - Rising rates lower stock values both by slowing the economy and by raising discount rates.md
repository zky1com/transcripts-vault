# Steve Eisman - Rising rates lower stock values both by slowing the economy and by raising discount rates

## Position
Steve Eisman argues that higher rates hurt stocks through both macroeconomic and valuation channels.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says tighter money raises borrowing costs and can slow the economy.
- He also explains that higher rates force higher discount rates in valuation models.
- He presents both mechanisms as reasons stocks fall when rates rise.

## Logic Chain
1. Higher rates can reduce economic activity and earnings expectations.
2. They also mechanically compress valuations through discount-rate math.
3. So stocks can be hit on both fundamentals and multiples when rates move up.
