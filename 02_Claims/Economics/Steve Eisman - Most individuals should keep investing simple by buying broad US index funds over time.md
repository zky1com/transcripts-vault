# Steve Eisman - Most individuals should keep investing simple by buying broad US index funds over time

## Position
Steve Eisman argues that most people should keep investing simple and systematic through broad US index funds.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript recommends the S&P 500 and possibly the NASDAQ for most individuals.
- He advises investing savings regularly instead of timing the market.
- He explicitly says to keep it simple.

## Logic Chain
1. Most people do not reliably beat the market.
2. Simpler portfolios reduce mistakes and decision fatigue.
3. So broad index exposure bought over time is a strong default strategy.
