# Chamath Palihapitiya - When AI creates unpriceable event risk the market responds by cutting multiples and raising the discount rate

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that when AI creates unpriceable event risk the market responds by cutting multiples and raising the discount rate.

## Evidence Cited
- He says holders of these equities want a much bigger margin of safety once AI risk becomes hard to price.
- He describes PE and revenue multiples getting cut sharply.
- He says the weighted average cost of capital rises because investors discount those cash flows much more aggressively.

## Logic Chain
1. When uncertainty cannot be modeled cleanly, investors demand more protection in price.
2. Chamath says that protection shows up as lower multiples and a higher discount rate.
3. That supports his claim about how markets respond to AI-driven event risk.
