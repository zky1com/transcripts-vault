# Steve Eisman - Long-term investing reduces the number of decisions and usually lowers the chance of major mistakes

## Position
Steve Eisman says long-term investing is often safer because it reduces how many consequential decisions an investor must make.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript contrasts buy-and-hold with constant trading.
- He says more decisions create more chances for error.
- He presents long-term investing as easier and better for most people.

## Logic Chain
1. Frequent trading multiplies the number of judgment calls.
2. More judgment calls create more opportunities for mistakes.
3. So long-term investing often lowers the risk of self-inflicted damage.
