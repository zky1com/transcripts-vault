# Chamath Palihapitiya - AI uncertainty forces investors to demand a much larger margin of safety before owning exposed stocks

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that AI uncertainty forces investors to demand a much larger margin of safety before owning exposed stocks.

## Evidence Cited
- He says investors now need a huge buffer if there is any chance an AI model could suddenly obliterate a business.
- He ties that directly to the repricing of once-expensive equities.
- He frames the move as rational protection against event risk rather than panic.

## Logic Chain
1. A larger margin of safety is the standard response to threats that can erase value abruptly.
2. Chamath says AI introduces exactly that kind of threat to some public companies.
3. That supports his claim that exposed stocks now require a much bigger buffer.
