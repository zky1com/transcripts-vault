# Chamath Palihapitiya - California's proposed billionaire wealth tax is mainly a political wedge that would drive capital out of the state

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that California's proposed billionaire wealth tax is mainly a political wedge that would drive capital out of the state.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the initiative is likely unconstitutional as written because property taxation must be uniform.
- He argues the real purpose is to bait opponents and weaponize them politically in the next election cycle.
- He says any serious attempt to extract wealth this way would push founders and capital out of California.

## Logic Chain
1. A tax that is legally weak but politically polarizing can still be useful as a signaling device.
2. Chamath says this initiative is designed more for that political function than for durable tax collection.
3. That supports his claim that the proposal is mainly a wedge and would accelerate capital flight.
