# Amy Butte - Roadshow preparation starts long before launch because investors should know the company before the formal pitch

## Position
Amy Butte says a good roadshow is not a blind first meeting because investor education should begin well before launch.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says the roadshow should not feel like a blind date.
- She describes meeting investors in non-deal roadshows before the formal process.
- She presents familiarity as part of the sale.

## Logic Chain
1. Investors are more comfortable when they already understand the company and management.
2. Formal roadshows are too compressed to build that from scratch.
3. So investor preparation needs to begin before the IPO marketing window opens.
