# Chamath Palihapitiya - AI is pushing investors from asking when cash flows get hit to asking whether those cash flows are durable at all

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that AI is pushing investors from asking when cash flows get hit to asking whether those cash flows are durable at all.

## Evidence Cited
- He says markets used to debate when businesses like Coca-Cola or Eli Lilly would face pressure.
- He says AI has shifted that conversation into an if question about whether the cash flows survive at all.
- He treats that shift as the core reason valuations are compressing.

## Logic Chain
1. Timing risk is easier to model than existence risk.
2. Chamath says AI has changed the market from pricing timing risk to pricing survival risk.
3. That supports his claim that investors are now questioning cash-flow durability itself.
