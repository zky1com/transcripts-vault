# Steve Eisman - Post-crisis regulation made the financial system safer but also created unintended consequences

## Position
Steve Eisman says post-crisis regulation made the financial system safer but also created unintended consequences.

## Evidence Cited
- Eisman says regulation after the crisis made the system much safer.
- He also says those changes produced unforeseen consequences.

## Logic Chain
1. Regulation reduced systemic fragility.
2. Large regulatory shifts also changed incentives and constraints elsewhere.
3. Therefore safer did not mean consequence-free.
