# David Friedberg - Knowledge work itself may prove to be a transitory economic phenomenon in the age of AI

## Position
David Friedberg argues that knowledge work itself may prove to be a transitory economic phenomenon in the age of AI.

## Evidence Cited
- He compares knowledge work to software-as-a-service as a phenomenon that may only exist in a particular technological window.
- He says AI could recast many knowledge workers into different activities very quickly.
- He treats that possibility as part of the broader demand-absorption problem.

## Logic Chain
1. If AI can perform a large share of knowledge work, the category may not remain economically central in its current form.
2. Friedberg says that possibility is now real enough to discuss directly.
3. That supports his claim that knowledge work may be transitory in the AI era.
