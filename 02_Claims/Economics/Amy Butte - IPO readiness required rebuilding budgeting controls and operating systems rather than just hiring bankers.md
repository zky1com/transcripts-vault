# Amy Butte - IPO readiness required rebuilding budgeting controls and operating systems rather than just hiring bankers

## Position
Amy Butte argues that real IPO preparation means rebuilding controls and systems, not merely starting the deal process.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript describes changing procurement, expense, and financial-control systems.
- She says the organization had to understand revenue, expenses, and planning in a new way.
- The discussion treats operational readiness as a core requirement.

## Logic Chain
1. Public companies need repeatable controls and reliable reporting.
2. Those capabilities often do not exist in immature private organizations.
3. So IPO readiness requires internal rebuilding before marketing the deal.
