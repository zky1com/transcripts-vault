# Steve Eisman - The market has become increasingly unmoored from the day-to-day lives of most Americans

## Position
Steve Eisman says that the market has become increasingly unmoored from the day-to-day lives of most Americans.

## Evidence Cited
- He says market leadership is concentrated in tech-heavy sectors.
- He contrasts that with the lived experience of ordinary consumers facing affordability pressure.

## Logic Chain
1. Market performance is increasingly driven by sectors not representative of most household conditions.
2. Many households face weak affordability despite rising indices.
3. Eisman therefore says the market is increasingly unmoored from everyday life.
