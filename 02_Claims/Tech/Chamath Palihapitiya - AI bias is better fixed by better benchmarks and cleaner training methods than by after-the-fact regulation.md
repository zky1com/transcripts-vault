# Chamath Palihapitiya - AI bias is better fixed by better benchmarks and cleaner training methods than by after-the-fact regulation

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that AI bias is better fixed by better benchmarks and cleaner training methods than by after-the-fact regulation.

## Evidence Cited
- He says current generalized benchmarks are overfit to a legacy worldview and need to be rewritten.
- He suggests more synthetic-data-based training and clearer disclosure of source weights.
- He warns that waiting for lawmakers to clean up bias after the fact will slow the market and distort development.

## Logic Chain
1. Bias that originates in training and evaluation is most efficiently corrected at the model-building stage.
2. Chamath says benchmark and data reform attack the source problem directly while regulation acts later and more crudely.
3. That supports his claim that better training methods beat after-the-fact regulation.
