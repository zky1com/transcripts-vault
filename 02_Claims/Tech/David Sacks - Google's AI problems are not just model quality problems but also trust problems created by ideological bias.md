# David Sacks - Google's AI problems are not just model quality problems but also trust problems created by ideological bias

## Position
David Sacks argues that Google's AI problems are not just model quality problems but also trust problems created by ideological bias.

## Evidence Cited
- He says incidents like biased outputs and DEI distortions damaged trust in Google's AI products.
- He links those problems to broader concerns about how training sources and company ideology shape outputs.
- He contrasts that with users' desire for models that behave as neutral tools rather than ideological editors.

## Logic Chain
1. Users judge AI systems not only on capability but on whether they trust the system's judgment and framing.
2. Sacks says Google damaged that trust through ideological distortions.
3. That supports his claim that Google's challenge is partly a trust problem, not just a technical one.
