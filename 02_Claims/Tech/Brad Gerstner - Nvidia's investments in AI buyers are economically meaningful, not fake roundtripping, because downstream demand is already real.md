# Brad Gerstner - Nvidia's investments in AI buyers are economically meaningful, not fake roundtripping, because downstream demand is already real

## Position
Brad Gerstner argues that Nvidia's investments in AI buyers are economically meaningful, not fake roundtripping, because downstream demand is already real.

## Evidence Cited
- He says there are no dark GPUs and the chips are being consumed rather than sitting idle.
- He notes that Nvidia's equity checks are tiny relative to its cash flow and do not force buyers to use Nvidia forever.
- He says the end demand for AI tokens applications and enterprise use is already substantial.

## Logic Chain
1. A transaction is not sham roundtripping if the product purchased is genuinely demanded and economically useful downstream.
2. Gerstner says Nvidia's AI-chip sales meet that substance test.
3. That supports his claim that these investments are real economic lubrication rather than fake revenue recycling.
