# David Sacks - State rules against algorithmic discrimination risk backdooring DEI ideology into AI models

## Position
David Sacks argues that state rules against algorithmic discrimination risk backdooring DEI ideology into AI models.

## Evidence Cited
- He says Colorado's standard would effectively require models to avoid outputs with disparate impact on protected groups.
- He connects that requirement to the same value-loading impulse that produced earlier DEI distortions in AI systems.
- He says similar state-level concepts are spreading beyond Colorado.

## Logic Chain
1. If a model is legally required to avoid politically disfavored output patterns across protected categories, its outputs must be ideologically filtered.
2. Sacks says that filter is effectively DEI by another name.
3. That supports his claim that these rules risk backdooring DEI ideology into AI models.
