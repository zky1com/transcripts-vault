# David Friedberg - Tesla FSD is close enough in practice that real robotaxis look plausible within a couple of years

## Position
David Friedberg argues that Tesla FSD is close enough in practice that real robotaxis look plausible within a couple of years.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the current FSD quality is extremely high with very few disengagements.
- He describes his own car as effectively already functioning like a robotaxi except for supervision requirements.
- He says the main remaining annoyance is not core driving competence but mandatory driver monitoring.

## Logic Chain
1. A supervised autonomy system that already handles most real driving successfully is much closer to commercial autonomy than a prototype.
2. Friedberg says Tesla is now in that near-commercial zone.
3. That supports his claim that real robotaxis are plausible within a couple of years.
