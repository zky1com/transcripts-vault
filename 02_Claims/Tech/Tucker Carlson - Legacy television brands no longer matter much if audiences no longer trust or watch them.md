# Tucker Carlson - Legacy television brands no longer matter much if audiences no longer trust or watch them

## Position
Tucker Carlson argues that buying old media brands will not reshape public opinion if those brands no longer command real audience trust.

## Evidence Cited
- Transcript says CBS and CNN are now mostly hollow brands.
- He argues that assembling larger media conglomerates does not make people consume or believe the product.
- He contrasts those brands with platforms like YouTube and X that still affect what people see.

## Logic Chain
1. Media influence depends on audience attention and trust.
2. Legacy TV brands have lost much of both.
3. So mergers around those brands matter less culturally than financiers assume.
