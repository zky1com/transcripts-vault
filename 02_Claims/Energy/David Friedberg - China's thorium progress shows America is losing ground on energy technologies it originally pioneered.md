# David Friedberg - China's thorium progress shows America is losing ground on energy technologies it originally pioneered

## Position
David Friedberg argues that China's thorium progress shows America is losing ground on energy technologies it originally pioneered.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the United States pioneered much of the underlying thorium-reactor research decades ago.
- He says China has now built and operated molten-salt thorium systems while the United States fenced itself in with regulation.
- He frames the result as part of a broader Chinese advantage in scaling next-generation energy systems.

## Logic Chain
1. Inventing a technology matters less than commercializing and scaling it.
2. Friedberg says America stalled while China carried thorium technology forward.
3. That supports his claim that the United States is losing ground on energy technologies it pioneered.
