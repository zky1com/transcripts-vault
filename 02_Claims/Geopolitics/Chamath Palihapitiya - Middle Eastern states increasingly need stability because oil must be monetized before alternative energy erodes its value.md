# Chamath Palihapitiya - Middle Eastern states increasingly need stability because oil must be monetized before alternative energy erodes its value

## Position
Chamath Palihapitiya argues that Middle Eastern states increasingly need stability because oil must be monetized before alternative energy erodes its value.

## Evidence Cited
- He says the region's core asset is oil and its value decays if it sits in the ground too long.
- He points to expanding alternatives such as gas solar and eventually abundant nuclear electricity.
- He says stability allows states to monetize reserves and reinvest the proceeds into new sectors.

## Logic Chain
1. Producers with a declining strategic asset have stronger incentives to convert it into capital while demand still exists.
2. Chamath says that pushes the region toward peace and stability over prolonged disorder.
3. That supports his claim that oil monetization pressure is now a stabilizing incentive.
