# David Sacks - Trump advanced the Gaza ceasefire by pressuring both Israel and Hamas instead of acting only as Israel's lawyer

## Position
David Sacks argues that Trump advanced the Gaza ceasefire by pressuring both Israel and Hamas instead of acting only as Israel's lawyer.

## Evidence Cited
- He says Trump was willing to coerce both sides rather than pressure only one side selectively.
- He cites commentary that previous US negotiators were often seen as representing Israel rather than mediating neutrally.
- He says that tougher posture helped produce a breakthrough on hostages aid and troop withdrawal.

## Logic Chain
1. A mediator can force movement only if both parties believe pressure will apply to them.
2. Sacks says Trump created that dynamic in a way previous administrations often did not.
3. That supports his claim that balanced pressure was central to the ceasefire breakthrough.
