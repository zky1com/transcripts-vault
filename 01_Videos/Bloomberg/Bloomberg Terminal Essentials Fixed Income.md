## Summary

This Bloomberg Terminal Essentials tutorial shows how the terminal can support a complete fixed-income workflow, from broad market monitoring to bond-level relative-value analysis. It begins with dashboards for spreads, sectors, currencies, and swap markets, then moves into natural-language bond search and fixed-income worksheets for filtering and charting universes of securities. It closes with bond comparison, yield-and-spread analysis, quote discovery, and inventory management tools that help users move from market context to tradeable pricing decisions.

## Key Insights

- The tutorial frames fixed-income work as a sequence from monitoring to security selection to quote execution.
- FICM and world bonds functions are positioned as the fastest ways to spot spread moves and anomalies worth investigating.
- BBTI gives a real-time view of swap and rate structures across multiple categories and currencies.
- Natural-language search lowers the friction of creating targeted bond universes.
- FIW becomes more useful when users export a screened universe into a worksheet for additional filtering and charting.
- CMBO is presented as a practical tool for judging a bond against similar issues.
- YAS matters because it lets the user test spread and yield assumptions interactively.
- ALQO and IM are the bridge from analysis into executable quote comparison and inventory management.
