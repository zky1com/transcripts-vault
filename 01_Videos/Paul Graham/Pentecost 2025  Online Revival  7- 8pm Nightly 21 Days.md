## Summary

In this opening Pentecost revival message, Paul Graham frames John 1 as the starting point for personal reset by insisting that Jesus is not merely a prophet or teacher but God himself. He argues that the creator understands human darkness because he made the one who is struggling, presents Christ as the light that darkness cannot overcome, and calls listeners to name the habits they need released from as the first act of revival honesty.

## Key Insights

- The sermon begins the reset series by grounding everything in the identity of Christ rather than in self-help.
- Paul repeatedly moves from creation theology to personal struggle, arguing that the maker is the one who can remake.
- Darkness is treated as real but not final because Christ's light remains stronger.
- The first practical step of revival is honest recognition of the habits that need to change.
