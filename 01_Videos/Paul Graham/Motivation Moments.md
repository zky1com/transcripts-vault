## Summary

This panel episode of Motivation Moments gathers Gregory Bloomfield with Craig Campbell, Paul Graham, Taylor Made Speaks, Chris Henderson, and Johanse Perry for a direct conversation about lust, temptation, integrity, and discipleship among men. The discussion treats lust as a common but dangerous struggle that thrives in silence, presses for honest mentorship instead of shame, and repeatedly returns to wisdom, accountability, marriage, and seeking God first as the path toward long-term integrity.

## Key Insights

- The panel normalizes struggle without excusing sin, which makes the conversation pastoral rather than performative.
- Several speakers stress that mentorship and honest conversation are essential because shame keeps men isolated.
- The episode frames wisdom and accountability as stronger long-term defenses than confidence or image.
- Gregory closes by pulling the whole discussion back to marriage, kingdom priorities, and practical integrity.
