## Summary

Paul Graham preaches Revelation 12 as a practical orientation for preparing for the final crisis, arguing that the real conflict is the great controversy between Christ and Satan rather than personal ego battles. He says preparation starts with choosing the winning side now, warns that church-state mixing corrupts truth, and frames end-time endurance around obedience, the blood of the Lamb, and the testimony of Jesus rather than fear-driven speculation.

## Key Insights

- The sermon shifts Revelation away from fear imagery toward a character-and-loyalty reading centered on Christ.
- Paul insists the great controversy is larger than personal religious tribalism and must be read from heaven downward.
- Preparation is framed as present obedience and spiritual identity, not merely decoding future events.
- The remnant theme is tied to the commandments of God and the testimony of Jesus rather than denominational confidence alone.
