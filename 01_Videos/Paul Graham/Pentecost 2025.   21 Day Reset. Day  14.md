## Summary

In this standard day-14 upload, Paul Graham again joins John 13 and John 14, but with a stronger emphasis on communion, humility, and revelation. He argues that Jesus first serves the disciples before calling them into service, that believers need more than partial washing if they want real communion with Christ, and that John 14 answers their confusion by showing that seeing Jesus is seeing the Father and that asking in Christ's name belongs inside the mission he is handing forward.

## Key Insights

- The sermon insists that Jesus models service before demanding it from others.
- Paul uses Peter's reaction to argue against partial religion and selective surrender.
- John 14 is treated as both revelation of the Father and reassurance for confused disciples.
- Prayer in Christ's name is framed as mission-shaped rather than merely self-directed.
