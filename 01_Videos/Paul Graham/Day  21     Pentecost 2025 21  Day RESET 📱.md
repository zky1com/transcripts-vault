## Summary

On day twenty-one, Paul Graham closes the reset series in John 21 by presenting the risen Jesus as the one who feeds, restores, and recommissions his disciples. He uses the disciples' empty nets, Jesus' breakfast by the shore, and Peter's threefold restoration to argue that revival is not the end of the story but the beginning of a life of obedient mission, shepherding, and deeper love for Christ.

## Key Insights

- Jesus often feeds and restores disciples before sending them back into mission.
- Obedience to Christ's voice turns empty effort into fruitful labor.
- Peter's restoration shows that failure does not disqualify someone from future service.
- Revival should lead to mission, care for people, and sustained discipleship.
