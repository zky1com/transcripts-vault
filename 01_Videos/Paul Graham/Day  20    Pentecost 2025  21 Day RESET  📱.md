## Summary

On day twenty, Paul Graham focuses on John 20 and treats the resurrection as both the foundation of Christian faith and the model for believers leaving their own tomb-like situations. He moves from Mary Magdalene's grief and confusion to Thomas's doubt, arguing that Jesus' risen presence turns mourners into messengers and calls believers to trust even when they do not yet have the proof they want.

## Key Insights

- The empty tomb is not only evidence about Jesus but a pattern for believers leaving dead spaces.
- Grief and confusion can coexist with Christ's presence when he is not yet recognized.
- Doubt can derail spiritual progress if it is allowed to rule the heart.
- Faith matures when people believe Christ's word before receiving all the proof they want.
