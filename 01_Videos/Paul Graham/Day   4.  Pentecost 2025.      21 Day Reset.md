## Summary

On day four of the 21 Day Reset series, Paul Graham walks through John 4 and presents the Samaritan woman's encounter with Jesus as a model of spiritual reset. He emphasizes that Jesus meets people directly in places of shame, names their hidden issues without rejecting them, offers living water that can truly satisfy, and teaches that real worship is grounded in spirit and truth rather than in a sacred location. The devotional ends by urging listeners to have their own well moment with Jesus through confession, grace, and renewal.

## Key Insights

- Jesus does not wait for people to clean themselves up before meeting them in their struggle.
- Public shame and private failure do not block Christ from extending grace and attention.
- Worship is relational and spiritual before it is geographical or institutional.
- A personal encounter with Jesus becomes the turning point for reset, renewal, and future change.
