## Summary

This service replay includes worship, announcements, testimony, and a guest message from Candita Hinds centered on unrealistic expectations. Hinds argues that much pain comes from demanding from people, money, or circumstances what they were never able to provide, uses vivid personal and relational examples to show how resentment and stress build, and redirects the congregation toward clearer communication, self-awareness, and trust in God as the only fully stable source of peace.

## Key Insights

- The core metaphor is that people create extra pain when they keep expecting a hamburger from a hardware store.
- Hinds treats resentment and disappointment as both emotional and bodily burdens rather than merely abstract feelings.
- The message rejects the idea that love, waiting, or overfunctioning can force other people to change.
- Her resolution is spiritual as well as practical: release misplaced expectations and root hope in God.

## Claims

- [[Candita Hinds - Unrealistic expectations create pain when people demand what others cannot provide]]
- [[Candita Hinds - People do not change simply because someone loves or waits for them]]
- [[Candita Hinds - Financial success alone cannot produce peace or emotional health]]
- [[Candita Hinds - Chronic stress from unrealistic self-expectations eventually affects the body]]
- [[Candita Hinds - Peace grows when expectations are shifted from people toward God]]
