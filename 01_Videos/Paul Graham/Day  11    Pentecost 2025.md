## Summary

On day eleven of the 21 Day Reset series, Paul Graham walks through John 11 and frames Lazarus' death and resurrection as proof that Jesus can turn delayed deliverance into a deeper revelation of faith. He argues that Christ sometimes allows grief, confusion, and apparent absence so that believers can see his glory more clearly, then uses Lazarus' call out of the tomb to urge listeners to come out of spiritual grave situations and trust Jesus as the resurrection and the life.

## Key Insights

- Divine delay does not mean divine absence; Jesus can be working toward a greater revelation.
- Jesus calls people out of dead places rather than leaving them trapped in old conditions.
- Resurrection language is meant to shape present faith, not only future hope.
- Coming out of the grave with Christ often invites new resistance, but it is still life-giving.
