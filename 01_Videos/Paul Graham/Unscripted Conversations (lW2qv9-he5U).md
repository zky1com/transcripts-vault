## Summary

This Unscripted panel starts with reactions to Sinners and expands into a conversation about spiritual discernment, the way churches package truth, and the long aftereffects of making sexuality taboo. By the end, the panel argues that sexual disconnection in marriage is usually not fixed by pressure or shame alone but by honesty, emotional reconnection, and intentional changes in environment and practice.

## Key Insights

- The panel distinguishes between rejecting a message and rejecting the way a message is packaged or delivered.
- Several speakers argue that church silence around sexuality leaves people to improvise instead of forming healthy expectations before marriage.
- The discussion treats sexual connection in marriage as shaped by stress, resentment, environment, and emotional safety rather than libido alone.
- Practical advice centers on honest conversation, reconnecting outside daily routine, and rebuilding intimacy before expecting desire to return.
