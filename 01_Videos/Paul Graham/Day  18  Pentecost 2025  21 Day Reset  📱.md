## Summary

On day eighteen, Paul Graham reflects on John 18 and uses Peter's denials, Judas' betrayal, and the arrest scene in Gethsemane to show how easily believers can collapse under pressure even after bold promises of loyalty. He emphasizes that Jesus still steps forward as the one to be taken, still protects his followers, and still offers restoration after failure, then challenges listeners not to trade Christ for their own Barabbas-like choices when confronted with fear, compromise, or social pressure.

## Key Insights

- Bold language about loyalty is not the same as quiet faithfulness under pressure.
- Jesus absorbs what should fall on his followers and remains their advocate even when they fail.
- Denial often happens in ordinary uncomfortable spaces rather than dramatic public moments.
- Failure is serious, but restoration remains possible because Christ still intercedes.
