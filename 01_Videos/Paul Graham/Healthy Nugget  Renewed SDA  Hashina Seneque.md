## Summary

Hashina Seneque presents brain care as a spiritual and lifestyle issue, arguing that the frontal lobe is central to judgment, worship, and intentional choice. She links sleep, food, movement, conversation, and mentally active habits to clearer thinking, warns that destructive habits can impair decision-making, and encourages the church to protect brain health as part of aging faithfully and vibrantly.

## Key Insights

- The talk treats the brain as a stewardship issue rather than only a medical topic.
- Seneque argues that many harmful thought patterns and weakened choices are reinforced by lifestyle, not just personality.
- She frames social connection, reading, games, music, and rest as practical ways to protect mental sharpness.
- The message repeatedly connects clear thinking with faithful Christian living.
