## Summary

On day six, Paul Graham uses John 6 and the feeding of the five thousand to argue that hunger is the place where Jesus meets people and resets discipleship. He emphasizes that the disciples were thinking in scarcity and status while Jesus was teaching service, shows that little resources become enough in Christ's hands, and frames the leftover baskets as a lesson that those who serve others are not left empty themselves.

## Key Insights

- The message treats spiritual hunger as an opportunity for Christ rather than a disqualifying weakness.
- Paul contrasts the disciples' scarcity mindset with Jesus' service-centered mission.
- The small lunch is presented as enough once it is surrendered into Christ's hands.
- The disciples are corrected from enjoying ministry status toward actually feeding people.
