## Summary

Paul Graham uses Hebrews 11 to describe faith as trusting God even when the outcome is unclear and the plan is not fully understood. He emphasizes that biblical figures acted by faith rather than by visible certainty, and urges listeners to bring that same trust into ordinary next steps, intercession for others, and daily obedience.

## Key Insights

- Faith is defined as trust in God's character more than confidence in a specific outcome.
- Hebrews 11 is used as a gallery of people who obeyed before seeing fulfillment.
- Ordinary acts like driving, working, signing papers, or speaking can become acts of faith.
- Interceding for other people is treated as part of exercising trust in God.
