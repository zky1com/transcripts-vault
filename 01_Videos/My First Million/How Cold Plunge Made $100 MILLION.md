## Summary

Sam gives the compressed version of Plunge's early growth story: a tiny cold-plunge startup created the appearance of momentum by delivering free units to recognizable health influencers before it had real scale. The clip's core lesson is that strategic seeding can create social proof powerful enough to make a brand look much bigger than it is at the start.

## Key Insights

- The company grew from a tiny base into nine-figure revenue in just a few years.
- Its early playbook centered on gifting units to well-known people rather than waiting for normal demand.
- The visible delivery tours made the brand look established before it actually was.
