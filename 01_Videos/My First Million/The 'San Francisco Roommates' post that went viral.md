## Summary

Sam Parr explains how his old roommate app got a major early growth spike without paid acquisition. The tactic was to make a hyper-local cultural infographic for San Francisco roommates, then distribute it through social platforms and by tagging the exact businesses and neighborhoods featured so the people being referenced would help spread it.

## Key Insights

- Parr emphasizes clickthrough advantage over broad reach by focusing on highly specific identity content.
- The creative worked because the jokes felt natively local rather than brand-authored.
- Tagging the featured stores and restaurants turned the infographic into a collaboration target instead of a cold post.
- The tactic mattered because the company had no meaningful launch budget.

- [[Sam Parr - Hyper-local identity content can outperform broad content because the intended audience clicks and shares at a much higher rate]]
