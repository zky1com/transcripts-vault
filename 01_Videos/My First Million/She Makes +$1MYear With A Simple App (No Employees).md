## Summary

Sam Parr uses the Rootd app as another example of a lean software business where the key advantage is solving a painful, specific problem well enough that distribution becomes mostly organic. The notable part of the story is not just the revenue, but that a single founder reached millions of downloads without leaning on paid acquisition or a large team.

## Key Insights

- The app spread because it addressed a concrete and painful user problem.
- Parr contrasts product-market fit with the default assumption that scale must come from paid ads.
- The one-person structure keeps the business unusually lean.
- The example reinforces his recurring interest in small, efficient software businesses.
