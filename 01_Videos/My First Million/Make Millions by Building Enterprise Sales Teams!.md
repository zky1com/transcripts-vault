## Summary

Sam Parr argues that an overlooked startup path is to build a strong enterprise sales organization first, then wrap software around it later if needed. His point is that trained sellers are scarce and valuable enough that a company organized around sales talent can become acquisition-worthy even before the product side is especially impressive.

## Key Insights

- Parr sees sales talent as the scarce asset, not the initial software.
- He recommends recruiting natural hustlers from nontraditional sales environments rather than defaulting to programmers.
- The business idea is essentially to professionalize raw selling ability into a B2B SaaS sales machine.
- He frames acqui-hire value as a realistic outcome if the team can reliably sell.
