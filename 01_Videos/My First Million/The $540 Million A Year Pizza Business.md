## Summary

This second Hunt Brothers clip highlights a different advantage in the model: the economics are structured to make adoption easier for the host store. Sam Parr points out that the business feels less like a traditional franchise and more like a lightweight operating system merchants can bolt on with limited downside.

## Key Insights

- The model lowers friction for store owners compared with a normal franchise agreement.
- Removing royalties and long contracts improves adoption incentives.
- The business scales because operators keep more flexibility while Hunt Brothers still controls supply.
- It is a good example of structuring incentives to widen distribution.
