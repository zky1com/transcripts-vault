## Summary

Nick Huber uses his recent mistakes to show what it looks like when confidence meets a tougher operating environment. The episode is strongest when he explains the gap between a good narrative and good business execution: buying companies, running portfolios, and owning real estate all become much harder when leverage, macro pressure, and operational complexity show up at the same time.

## Key Insights

- Huber's early success made business look easier and more transferable than it actually was.
- The somewhere.com acquisition became a real education in structure, leverage, execution, and macro risk.
- His current thinking is more cyclical and operationally grounded than his earlier hot-hand mindset.
- He now sees systems, patience, and location strategy as more durable edges than hustle theater.
