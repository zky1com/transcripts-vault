## Summary

Sam Parr describes a simple distribution move that helped drive roughly one hundred thousand users for his roommate app. Instead of buying reach, the team created a culturally specific San Francisco roommates infographic and engineered the post so the very businesses and neighborhoods being joked about would feel included and amplify it.

## Key Insights

- The tactic worked because it piggybacked on local identity rather than generic startup branding.
- Parr chose a format people already knew how to consume and reshare.
- Tagging referenced businesses turned the content into ego bait for local participants.
- The example highlights how far distribution creativity can go when budget is missing.
