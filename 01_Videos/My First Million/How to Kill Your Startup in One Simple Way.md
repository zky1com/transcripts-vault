## Summary

Sam Parr argues that founders often think variety looks impressive when it actually signals weakness. In his view, too many revenue streams or acquisition channels usually mean the company has not found one dominant engine that really works.

## Key Insights

- He treats multiple weak channels as a red flag rather than as evidence of diversification.
- The clip frames strong companies as businesses that double down on one channel and one monetization engine first.
- Facebook is used as the example of a company that won by finding one dominant distribution loop and one clear monetization path.

One of the biggest poker tales with a business is when they have seven revenue streams. They think they come in puffing their chest being like, "Look, we've got seven."
