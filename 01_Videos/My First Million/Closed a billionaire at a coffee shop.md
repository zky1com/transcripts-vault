## Summary

Jesse Itzler tells a compact origin story about forcing his way into a room full of qualified prospects by controlling a tiny but valuable point of friction. The lesson is not really about muffins; it is about getting physically close to the right buyers and manufacturing a conversation starter when you do not yet have brand, access, or process.

## Key Insights

- He targeted a place where his exact buyers would naturally gather instead of trying to market broadly.
- The tactic worked because it created a memorable opening, not because it was scalable in itself.
- His first sale came from proximity and improvisation rather than from a formal funnel.
