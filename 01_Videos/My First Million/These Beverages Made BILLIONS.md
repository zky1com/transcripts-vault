## Summary

Sam Parr uses Prime and Alani New to argue that outsider founders can create enormous beverage businesses when they already know how to capture attention and build brand momentum. His point is less about drinks specifically than about the scale of value that can be created when creator distribution and consumer-product execution meet.

## Key Insights

- Parr rejects the idea that the founders were random novices who stumbled into the category.
- He frames sneaker reselling as an early training ground in taste, hype, and demand creation.
- The Kim Kardashian partnership is presented as evidence of deliberate brand-building skill, not luck.
- Prime looks even more impressive in his telling because it followed an already large prior success with Alani New.
