## Summary

Daniel Negreanu's startup advice is really a mindset point about ownership. He argues that the right way to approach an early-stage opportunity is to work as if you own the whole thing and trust that the right founders will expand your upside if your contribution truly matters.

## Key Insights

- Negreanu downplays the psychological trap of obsessing over a small initial percentage.
- He treats ownership mentality as a behavior first and a cap-table outcome second.
- His story suggests that value creation can sometimes be rewarded more generously than people expect.
- The real advice is to optimize for learning, contribution, and aligned people rather than immediate negotiation.

## Claims

- [[Daniel Negreanu - Treating a startup role like you own 100% can matter more than obsessing over a small initial equity percentage]]
