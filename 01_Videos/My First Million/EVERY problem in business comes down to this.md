## Summary

This short clip reduces most business problems to a simple diagnostic split: either demand is too low or supply is too constrained. Once that diagnosis is clear, Sam's prescription is practical rather than abstract: bring in people who are better than you at the bottlenecked part of the business.

## Key Insights

- He treats diagnosis as the main unlock, because supply and demand problems require opposite responses.
- His leadership point is that scaling often means hiring strength where you are weak, not trying to personally solve every bottleneck.

- [[Sam Parr - Most business problems are either demand constraints or supply constraints]]
