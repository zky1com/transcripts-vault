## Summary

This clip compresses the same Amit Agarwal example into a sharper wealth lesson: a one-person software company can generate economics comparable to an organization valued in the hundreds of millions. Sam Parr's emphasis is on how concentrated ownership changes the meaning of revenue and profit for a tiny software business.

## Key Insights

- Parr highlights owner economics rather than startup optics.
- A tiny software business can create large wealth when nearly all profits remain concentrated.
- The example challenges the assumption that a serious business must have a large team.
- The real appeal is not just revenue size but the value of concentrated income.
