## Summary

Jesse Cole explains that many of the Savannah Bananas' early wins came from shameless attention experiments rather than polished conventional marketing. The clip is a compact argument for using outrageous, talk-worthy ideas to break through when no one is paying attention to you yet.

## Key Insights

- Cole was optimizing for attention first, not dignity or industry convention.
- The examples are intentionally bizarre because memorability was the goal.
- He treated themed stunts as a practical growth tool rather than a side joke.
- The clip reflects a willingness to test many weird ideas in public until something spreads.
