## Summary

Sam Parr uses Amit Agarwal as an example of how a one-person software business can become enormous by building simple utility plugins inside an ecosystem with built-in distribution. The lesson is less about coding novelty and more about finding repetitive user demand, pairing freemium distribution with paid upgrades, and concentrating the economics inside a tiny operation.

## Key Insights

- Agarwal's business shows how a narrow utility can scale when it sits inside a huge software ecosystem.
- Freemium distribution plus paid feature upgrades can support meaningful revenue without a large team.
- One plugin can become substantial on its own, and a portfolio of plugins compounds that effect.
- The business works because profit stays concentrated rather than spread across a large organization.
