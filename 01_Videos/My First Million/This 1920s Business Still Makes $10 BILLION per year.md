## Summary

Sam Parr highlights Cintas as a classic example of how durable wealth gets created through plain operational services rather than flashy innovation. His emphasis is on the revenue quality: the company wins by becoming part of the routine maintenance layer of business customers and turning that into long-term, repeatable cash flow.

## Key Insights

- The company became more valuable as it embedded itself into routine business maintenance.
- Operational trust created room to move from one service into multiple supplies and compliance-related needs.
- The story shows how a business can feel invisible to outsiders while still becoming massive.
- Recurring contracts matter as much as the individual product being sold.
