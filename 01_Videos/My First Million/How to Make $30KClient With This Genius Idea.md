## Summary

Sam Parr outlines a simple local-service opportunity around sport courts. The business logic is straightforward: if customers are paying a huge markup to a branded installer, an operator can unbundle the job, use subcontractors to lower cost, then resell the finished dream at a still-healthy price with Facebook lead generation on top.

## Key Insights

- The business idea came from first-hand quote-shopping rather than from abstract trend research.
- Sam's margin insight came from seeing how much cheaper subcontracted delivery was than full branded installation.
- He reframes the offer as selling backyard fun and a wow-factor upgrade rather than just concrete and paint.
- Meta lead generation is presented as the obvious first customer channel for this kind of local offer.
