## Summary

Sam Parr explains how his first startup, Bunk, got early usage before it was even an app. The core move was not sophisticated software. It was manually packaging existing roommate supply into a simpler listing format that prospective renters already understood, then using Craigslist distribution to pull people into the funnel.

## Key Insights

- Bunk found early traction before it had product complexity by starting with a simple Weebly website.
- The initial supply came from landlords with larger apartments rather than from individual roommate seekers.
- Sam reframed shared apartments as one-bedroom style listings to make the offer easier to click.
- Beautiful Craigslist listings worked as the early distribution channel that pulled renters into the Bunk site.
