## Summary

Sam Parr tells the rise-and-fall arc of Oleg Tinkoff as both a banking story and a personal courage story. What begins as a sharp example of buying and scaling a bank into a digital giant ends as an example of how founder wealth can evaporate quickly when political power turns against the person who built it.

## Key Insights

- The business story starts with acquisition and transformation rather than founding a bank entirely from zero.
- Goldman's involvement is used as a signal of how large and legitimate the bank became.
- Political exposure became more decisive than business execution once the war changed the context.
- Parr keeps the focus on personal outspokenness as the dramatic center of the story.
