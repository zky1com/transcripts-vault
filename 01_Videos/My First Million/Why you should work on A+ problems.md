## Summary

Shaan Puri uses the Twitch-Ninja crisis as a lesson in strategic prioritization. His point is that career acceleration often comes from attaching yourself to the most important problem in the room rather than staying dutifully inside your original job box when a clearly bigger issue appears.

## Key Insights

- Puri frames problem selection as a leverage decision rather than a hustle decision.
- He treats initiative as something you can demonstrate inside a big company, not just as a founder trait.
- The memo mattered because it translated urgency into a specific recommendation rather than just commentary.
- His example shows that access often follows visible contribution on priority problems.

## Claims

- [[Shaan Puri - Career leverage comes from finding the A+ problem and making yourself useful on it immediately]]
