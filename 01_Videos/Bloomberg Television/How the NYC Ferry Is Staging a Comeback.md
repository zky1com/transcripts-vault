This is a story about the NYC Ferry nearly a decade into its existence. What's gone right, what's gone wrong, and what's still to come. The ferry is run by New York City's Economic Development Corporation or EDC. Critics of the ferry point out that there's a gap between how much a passenger pays for a ride and how much it costs the city to take them on that ride. As of 2025, the city was subsidizing each ride on the NYC ferry by $8.33. >> So, our subsidy is down considerably, almost 40% from its peak.
