large scale industrial processes. But many of today's working robots are

Just 13,000 humanoids were shipped in 2025, compared with over half a million
industrial robots installed in a single year.
Projections show that could change rapidly, with as many as 12 million

population, China is already leading in volume, producing the majority of
humanoids today, with prices far below Western peers.
Widespread adoption still faces major hurdles, so for now, the gap between
hype and reality remains wide. But if even a fraction of these
forecasts materialise, humanoids could move from niche machines to a defining
force in the global economy.
