Gainesboro is a tight-knit community.

The company that comes into Gainesboro, Ridgerunner, starts to buy up large

Christians. Ridgerunner is working in partnership
with a venture capital firm called New Founding.

called the end of a civilizational cycle, a loss of trust in institutions.

"big sort," where as Americans, we are kind of sorting ourselves into enclaves
