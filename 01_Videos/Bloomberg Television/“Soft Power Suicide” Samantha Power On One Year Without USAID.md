We're a year away from the dissolution, the end of USAID. You've called it the ravaging of USAID. Villages that won't be electrified. USAID through its Power Africa program helped connect about 150 million people to electricity. Most of that was in places that would never have been able to connect to the grid in my lifetime. Pop-up solar panels, uh you know, private public partnerships uh that allowed things to happen in a much more accelerated pace. So, the soft power suicide for the United States I think is pretty obvious when you pull the rug out from programming of this nature with no warning, with no regard for the human consequences of those actions.
