## Summary

Chamath argues that AI is changing equity valuation from a timing problem into an existence problem. Once markets stop asking when a business might be pressured and start asking whether its cash flows remain durable at all, they respond by cutting multiples, raising discount rates, and demanding much larger buffers before owning the stock.

## Key Insights

- Chamath says AI is pushing investors out of a normal timing debate and into a harder durability debate.
- He frames AI as creating event risk that public markets do not know how to price cleanly.
- He argues the practical result is lower earnings multiples, lower revenue multiples, and higher discount rates.

## Key Quotes

> This is no longer a when moment.

> The market is very much in an if mode.
