## Summary

Bipul Sinha argues that Rubrik is benefiting from the AI wave because enterprises need visibility, control, and availability over their data before they can safely deploy AI agents. He frames Rubrik's moat as mission-critical data infrastructure built through years of enterprise feedback, and says geopolitical conflict is increasing demand for cyber resilience as digital infrastructure becomes a strategic target.

## Key Insights

- Rubrik positions itself as an enabler of enterprise AI deployment rather than a company threatened by AI commoditization.
- Sinha argues that mission-critical infrastructure software gains durability from years of production use and customer feedback, not just feature breadth.
- He treats cyber resilience as part of national and economic infrastructure because attacks increasingly target data systems and AI environments.
- Geopolitical escalation can translate into near-term security spending as firms try to keep core systems operating during retaliatory cyber attacks.
