## Summary

Kate Rooney reports that Anthropic's revenue momentum continued even during its Pentagon dispute, with annualized revenue nearing $20 billion and much of the acceleration tied to Claude Code. At the same time, she says the same dispute now threatens future growth because defense and startup customers are already considering or executing offboarding decisions in response to the government's supply-chain-risk designation.

## Key Insights

- Anthropic's coding product is positioned as a major commercial driver, not a side feature.
- The Pentagon fight is no longer just reputational; it is beginning to shape real customer behavior.
- Anthropic's integration depth with customers creates both revenue momentum and painful switching dynamics.
- The company's near-term growth and its political risk are now moving in opposite directions at the same time.
