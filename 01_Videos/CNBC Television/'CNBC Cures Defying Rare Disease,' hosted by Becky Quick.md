## Summary

In this preview for CNBC Cures: Defying Rare Disease, Becky Quick frames the documentary as both a personal story about her daughter Kaylee's rare genetic disease and a broader look at families pushing for diagnosis, gene therapy, and cures under emotionally and financially extreme conditions. The clip emphasizes that rare-disease medicine is advancing quickly, but that access, diagnosis, and the cost of actually delivering these breakthroughs remain the hardest obstacles for many families.

## Key Insights

- The preview is anchored emotionally in Becky Quick's role as both journalist and mother.
- It presents rare-disease advocacy as a mix of grief, urgency, fundraising, and scientific hope.
- Gene therapy is shown not as speculative future medicine but as a near-term intervention already confronting families with hard choices.
- The strongest structural theme is that the bottlenecks now are often cost, diagnosis, and execution rather than basic scientific imagination.
