## Summary

Pete Stavros says KKR's recent services deal succeeded because the firm bought into a business tied to the data-center boom and then scaled it aggressively alongside management. He describes today's exit market as far less competitive than the frenzy of 2021 and 2022, but argues KKR is still positioned for a record year of exits because it avoided overexposure during the hottest vintage years and deployed capital more evenly across cycles.

## Key Insights

- Stavros says the deal combined strong market timing with substantial operational execution rather than relying only on multiple expansion.
- He describes today's broader exit market as more buyer-friendly and less crowded than during the private-market boom.
- He expects KKR to post a record exit year because its disciplined deployment process avoided concentration in the most overpriced vintages.
