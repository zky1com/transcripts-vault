## Summary

Mackenzie Sigalos explains Anthropic's injunction hearing as a high-stakes fight over both immediate revenue and long-run precedent. She argues that if the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation survives in court, it could give future administrations a powerful tool to freeze domestic tech companies out of government business during contract disputes, while the uncertainty itself is already pushing customers toward competitors.

## Key Insights

- The legal fight is framed as both a contract issue and a structural precedent about state power over domestic AI vendors.
- Claude's integration depth makes offboarding costly, but uncertainty is still pushing customers toward safer alternatives.
- Even if Anthropic wins legally, competitors may already have captured some contracts permanently.
- The case matters beyond Anthropic because cloud and AI vendors are watching how far national-security designations can be stretched.
