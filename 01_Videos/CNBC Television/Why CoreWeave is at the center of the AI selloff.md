What started as a crypto company in suburban New Jersey is now a key Nvidia partner and sort of a bellwether for the health and durability of the AI trade. CoreWeave's business model involves renting out GPU space in the cloud to customers across industries. To source that compute CoreWeave in turn has to source those GPUs from the major players in the chip space like Nvidia.
