## Summary

This CNBC livestream combines two conversations: one with Ramp chief economist Ara Karazian on Anthropic's surge in enterprise adoption, and another with Axiom founder Karina Li on using math and formal verification to push frontier AI into higher-value domains. Together, the segment argues that the AI market is shifting from generic chatbot novelty toward more embedded enterprise workflows and toward specialized systems that solve hard verification and reasoning problems.

## Key Insights

- Karazian says Anthropic has moved from a niche engineer favorite to the preferred enterprise starting point for many new AI buyers.
- He argues that distribution, product positioning, and workflow integration matter as much as raw benchmark quality in enterprise adoption.
- Li frames mathematics as a clean proving ground for frontier reasoning techniques that can later unlock code and verification products.
- The segment treats enterprise trust and context integration as the real bottleneck between casual AI use and system-level business productivity.
