## Summary

Ed Yardeni argues that the market's recent selloff in software and large-cap AI winners reflects an overreaction after earlier AI euphoria rather than a durable breakdown in the broader bull market. He favors rotating away from the Magnificent Seven and into other sectors and overseas markets, while warning that software now requires selective stock picking because AI will create both winners and losers inside the category.

## Key Insights

- Yardeni sees the unwind in AI enthusiasm as a healthy reset rather than the end of the bull market.
- He believes broad software exposure is too blunt because AI disruption will hit companies unevenly.
- His preferred posture is rotation rather than panic, especially toward financials, energy, staples, and non-US markets.
- He treats current market behavior as a swing from AI euphoria to AI fatigue and fear.
