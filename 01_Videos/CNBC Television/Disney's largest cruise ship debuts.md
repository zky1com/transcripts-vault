The Disney Cruise Line is about to get bigger, a lot bigger. This month, the company's eighth and largest ship, the Disney Adventure, will embark on its maiden voyage in Southeast Asia. The vessel accommodates a whopping 6,700 passengers, about 2/3 more than the capacity on Disney's Wishclass ships. Not only is this Disney's largest cruise ship, it's also its first to sail in Asia. The Disney Adventure will make its birth in Singapore and provide guests with three and four voyages at sea. This expansion into the region comes at a time when fewer international travelers are putting the US on their travel itineraries. While tourism grew worldwide last year, the United States was the only major destination to see a decline in foreign visitors. Those declines have continued this year. In addition to travel bans, President Donald Trump's administration has also introduced a $250 visa fee for many tourists and signed off on more rigorous vetting, including searches of electronic devices. Disney's experiences division, which includes its cruise line, theme parks, and resorts, has long been a global business, and it's a booming one. While domestic destinations like Disneyland and Walt Disney World drive the majority of revenue, international is growing steadily, and the company sees Asia as an untapped market. Already, Disney has Tokyo Disney Resort, Hong Kong Disneyland, and Shanghai Disney Resort in the market. However, the southern part of this region was not being serviced. That's where the Disney Adventure comes in. Initially designed to be a floating casino, the ship went up for sale partway through its construction when its parent company went bankrupt in 2022. That's when Disney swooped in and bought it. The unique opportunity not only bolstered Disney's cruise line with another vessel, but allowed the company to move into the Southeast Asia region much quicker. Disney has tailored the adventure with these passengers in mind. That includes a shopping and gaming area based on the fictional city San Francio from Big Hero 6, and diverse dining options inspired by The Jungle Book, Moana, and Lilo and Stitch. For the adventure, the company tapped into its wealth of IP, including Aladdin, Toy Story, Tangled, Frozen, and Marvel. There's also the first ever Duffy and Friends store at Sea, a National Geographic shop, and beverages from the Singapore-based TWWGT company. And for this particular customer, which tends to prefer multigenerational travel, yes, kids, grandkids, adults, and grandparents, Disney has designed the ship to allow for these groups to separate or to get in on the fun together. And there's more to come after the adventure set sail. Disney is set to launch five more ships between 2027 and 2031.
