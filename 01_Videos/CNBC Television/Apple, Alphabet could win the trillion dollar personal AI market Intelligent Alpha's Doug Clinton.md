## Summary

Doug Clinton argues that investors should look beyond Nvidia toward Apple, Alphabet, and Amazon, with his strongest upside thesis centered on Apple and Google as emerging winners in a potential personal-AI market. He says that market is not yet priced into those stocks, sees Apple as especially attractive because it combines upside optionality with strong free cash flow, and expects large enterprise software names to be more defensible than smaller SaaS vendors as AI agents spread.

## Key Insights

- Clinton distinguishes between the already-priced hardware AI trade and a newer personal-AI story he thinks the market still underestimates.
- He views Apple as unusually attractive because it is not spending hyperscaler-level capex yet still retains large AI upside if Siri execution improves.
- He expects Nvidia's earnings reaction to be more muted because expectations are already high.
- He sees the biggest software resilience in large enterprise deployments that are harder for AI-native alternatives to replace quickly.
