## Summary

This Crypto World episode examines why Bitcoin was trading more like a mainstream risk asset even as its long-term adoption story remained intact, with Rick Edelman arguing that the asset's correlation profile is changing because institutional ownership is expanding. Mackenzie Sagallos also covers Binance's defamation suit against the Wall Street Journal, Wells Fargo's new crypto trademark push, and Carlos Domingo's view that regulators have now done much of the foundational work needed for tokenized securities to scale.

## Key Insights

- Edelman treats Bitcoin's growing correlation with risk assets as evidence of mainstreaming rather than thesis failure.
- He argues Bitcoin's surviving use case is now store-of-value exposure with strong brand dominance, not transactional currency replacement.
- The tokenized-securities segment frames recent U.S. guidance as a parity breakthrough rather than a marginal technical update.
- The show ties traditional financial firms' crypto moves back to improving regulatory clarity and product confidence.
