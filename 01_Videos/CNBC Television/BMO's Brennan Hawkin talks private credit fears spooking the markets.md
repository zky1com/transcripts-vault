## Summary

Brennan Horgan says investors are focusing on private credit because that is where loan growth has been strongest and therefore where many expect the next credit problems to surface. He argues that losses are inevitable in credit portfolios but should be viewed separately from redemption and flow pressure, notes that non-traded BDCs are more liquid than old non-traded REIT structures because their loan books roll faster, and warns that asset-based finance may actually be where some of the first real cracks are appearing.

## Key Insights

- Horgan is not dismissing private-credit risks; he is trying to distinguish expected credit losses from panic-driven flow stress.
- He sees the sector's rapid growth as the reason investors are now treating it as the likely epicenter of a credit turn.
- The interview highlights how redemption mechanics and portfolio losses are separate but mutually reinforcing problems.
- He also suggests investors may be looking in the wrong corner if they focus only on direct lending and ignore asset-based finance.
