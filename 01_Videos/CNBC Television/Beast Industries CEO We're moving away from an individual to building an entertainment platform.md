## Summary

Beast Industries' CEO says the company is evolving from a business centered on Jimmy Donaldson into a broader entertainment platform designed to support new talent, more formats, and a wider set of businesses. He also outlines how the company is trying to manage prediction-market abuse, why it bought Step to make financial literacy engaging for young audiences, and how it sees crypto infrastructure like Ethereum and stablecoins fitting into lower-cost consumer finance over time.

## Key Insights

- The company is trying to turn creator popularity into a platform with multiple characters, formats, and monetization channels.
- Jeff treats prediction-market abuse as a structural governance problem that requires both internal controls and regulatory clarity.
- The Step acquisition is framed as a mission-aligned way to turn financial literacy into entertainment for Gen Z and Gen Alpha.
- He links Beast Industries' reach to long-term share-of-wallet expansion across media, products, and finance.
