## Summary

Deirdre Bosa argues that Apple is mishandling the rise of vibe-coding apps by blocking or freezing tools that expand software creation to ordinary users. She says Apple is using a safety rationale inconsistently and risks pushing the next generation of AI-native builders to the web, where Apple loses App Store control, monetization, and relevance.

## Key Insights

- Bosa frames vibe coding as a direct continuation of Apple's original democratizing-computing mission, not as a fringe app category.
- She sees Apple's current policy as internally inconsistent because other browser-like or model-based apps already enable similarly open-ended behavior.
- The real strategic risk is not just developer frustration but losing the builder layer of the next AI era outside the App Store.
- She suggests the policy may reflect internal confusion between App Store and Xcode incentives rather than a coherent long-term strategy.
