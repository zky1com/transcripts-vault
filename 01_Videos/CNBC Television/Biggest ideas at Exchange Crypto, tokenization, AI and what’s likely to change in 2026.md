## Summary

A panel at Exchange 2026 argues that the ETF business is entering a new phase driven less by simple passive exposure and more by active strategies, crypto access, real-asset diversification, and eventual tokenization. The panelists describe crypto indexing as a mass-market on-ramp, say AI is reshaping both portfolio construction and product development, and broadly agree that tokenization is inevitable but still needs clearer regulation and stronger market plumbing before it can become mainstream.

## Key Insights

- The panel treats the ETF wrapper as a delivery technology that can now host many more content types and outcomes than simple passive beta.
- Crypto, tokenization, and AI are presented as connected themes because each changes distribution, settlement, and portfolio design.
- Several speakers stress that real assets and commodities may be underappreciated second-order beneficiaries of AI infrastructure buildout.
- Tokenization is viewed as transformative in the long run, but the panel is still focused on proving real use cases and operational readiness.
