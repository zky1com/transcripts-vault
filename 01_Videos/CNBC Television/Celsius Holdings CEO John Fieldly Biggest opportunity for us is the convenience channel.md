## Summary

John Fieldly says Celsius is now operating with a true portfolio approach, using Celsius, Alani Nu, and Rockstar to reach different energy-drink consumers while leveraging Pepsi's distribution more tightly than before. He argues the biggest white-space opportunity remains the convenience channel, says Alani Nu is expanding the category by bringing in more female consumers, and frames the company's broader growth formula as getting more people in more places to consume the products more often, with international expansion as the next leg of the story.

## Key Insights

- Fieldly presents Celsius less as a single-brand story and more as a multi-brand growth platform tied to Pepsi distribution.
- He sees channel expansion and shelf-space wins as the near-term operating lever, especially in convenience stores.
- The interview highlights how Celsius wants different brands to cover distinct consumer cohorts instead of cannibalizing one another.
- He also points to mocktails, mealtime use, and fizz-free products as evidence that energy drinks are becoming broader lifestyle beverages.
