## Summary

Mackenzie Sigalos argues that a Globalstar acquisition would matter less for raw satellite count than for the licenses, operational history, and direct-to-device capabilities Amazon badly needs in its effort to challenge Starlink. She frames the report as a sign that Amazon's satellite ambitions remain alive but still operationally behind, with deployment deadlines and launch delays making licensing and spectrum assets especially valuable.

## Key Insights

- Globalstar's strategic value is framed as regulatory and technical leverage, not scale.
- Amazon's satellite effort is still capacity-constrained and deadline-sensitive relative to Starlink's lead.
- Direct-to-device connectivity is becoming one of the most strategically important battlegrounds in satellite telecom.
- Any such acquisition would also be shaped by Apple's stake and by the FCC timeline around deployment obligations.
