Michigan has not elected a Republican to the U.S. Senate since 1994,
but this year, Republican Mike Rogers is
trying to change that as he faces off against Democrat
Abdul El-Sayed for the open seat left by retiring
Senator Gary Peters. This race is part of a
broader fight for control of Congress. Michigan won't
decide that fight on its own, but its Senate contest
and competitive House races could help shape the
outcome. Michigan is a bellwether
state. President Trump won here in
2024 by less than two percentage points. He lost
to Biden in the state in 2020 by less than three
points heading into Election Day. The economy,
cost of living and corruption poll as top
issues for Michigan voters. A recent poll found nearly
half of likely Michigan voters say they are worse
off financially than they were a year ago. 85% of
those polled say they felt corporations and the wealthy
have too much influence over government. The race between
El-Sayed and Rogers is one of the most closely watched
