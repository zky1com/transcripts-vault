## Summary

Howard Marks says direct hands-on use of Claude convinced him that AI's capabilities are already far more powerful than many people appreciate. He believes AI can eliminate a large share of knowledge work and may move faster than society can adjust, creating labor-market disruption even if the long-run employment outcome remains uncertain.

## Key Insights

- Marks treats personal experimentation with frontier models as the fastest way to understand the technology's real capabilities.
- He does not claim certainty about long-run employment offsets, which makes his labor warning more about transition speed than pure technological unemployment.
- His concern is that AI adoption may outrun society's retraining and adjustment mechanisms.
- He sees the technology as improving so quickly that any static view of its limits is likely to age badly.
