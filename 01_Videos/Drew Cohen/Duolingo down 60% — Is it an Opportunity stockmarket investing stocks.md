## Summary

This short Duolingo note is a compact valuation thought exercise rather than a full business teardown. Drew Cohen focuses on how investors should think about a large price drawdown: whether the market has genuinely broken the thesis or simply reset expectations to a level that offers more attractive long-term return potential.

## Key Insights

- A stock falling sharply is not evidence by itself that the underlying business is broken.
- The right question after a drawdown is what has changed in business quality versus valuation.
- Consumer education software needs to be judged on durability of growth and acquisition efficiency.
